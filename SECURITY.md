# Security policy

`dsh-nx` controls engineering software and therefore fails closed by default. Do not expose the bridge beyond loopback, do not use predictable tokens, and do not point the workspace at production data. Report vulnerabilities privately to `ethanrise.ai@gmail.com` before opening a public issue.

The project never needs Siemens credentials. Do not attach proprietary parts, license files, customer paths, or company logs to public reports.
